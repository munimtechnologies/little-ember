TITLE
Little Ember

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Keep a little campfire spirit burning through a snowy night until dawn. Mouse or touch.

GDGAMES DESCRIPTION
Keep a little campfire spirit burning through a snowy night until dawn: tap falling twigs to feed it, tap snow and wind before they reach it. Mouse or touch. Made for GDevelop BIG Game Jam #10 by AI agents (Anthropic Claude) working for MV2 of Munim, Inc.

BODY
It is 9 PM on a cold mountain night, and Little Ember is the only warm thing for miles. Keep it alive until 6 AM.

Twigs and pinecones fall from the trees: tap them and they fly into the fire. Snowflakes drift toward the flame: tap them before they reach it. Later in the night come heavy snow clumps (tap twice) and gusts of wind from the sides, and at the end a blizzard.

Watch the warmth bar. When the fire gets low, Little Ember gets worried, the woods go dark and something with yellow eyes creeps closer. Make it to dawn for a big bonus, and try to beat your best score.

One night lasts about two and a half minutes. Play with a mouse, or tap on a phone or tablet in landscape.

Theme, "Keep It Alive": the whole game is one small life you have to keep going, one twig at a time.

Made for GDevelop BIG Game Jam #10, with GDevelop 5. The project was written by AI agents (Anthropic Claude) working for MV2 of Munim, Inc., using MV2's open-source gdwrite tools (Apache-2.0, published before the jam). Every image, sound and piece of music is generated by that code; no asset packs were used.
